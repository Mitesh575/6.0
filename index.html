Symposium Lifecycle

Event Roadmap & Schedule

  • 01 Registration Portal: Open & Close

    Online registration opens for all 6 specialized technical arenas. Squads and participants must lock in their registrations before the official portal closing deadline.

  • 02 Inauguration & Chief Guest Felicitation

    The symposium officially commences at Sri Sairam Engineering College with ceremonial lamp lighting, keynote addresses by industry dignitaries, and chief guest felicitation.

  • 03 Hall & Lab Venue Allotment

    Following the ceremony, dedicated computer labs, high-performance computing clusters, and presentation halls are announced, guiding participants to their respective arena tracks.

  • 04 Arena Battles — September 26, 2026

    All technical arenas run concurrently in their assigned halls. Each event conducts its independent rounds, challenges, and evaluations to determine its own 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners.

  • 05 Valedictory & Prize Distribution

    Grand closing ceremony celebrating champions across every arena with cash awards from the ₹19,000+ prize pool, trophies, and official certificates of excellence.
