01 What to Expect

  • ₹10,000 Virtual Credits: Every registered squad starts with ₹10,000 in virtual credits to allocate across the auction floor.
  • Strategic Tool Bidding: Compete against other teams in live bidding wars for frontend, backend, AI models, and database modules.
  • Credit Management: Balance aggressive acquisitions with fiscal discipline to avoid running out of funds before critical stack components.
  • Surprise Real-World Problem: After auction closure, receive a surprise domain problem statement.
  • Strict Tool Constraint: Architect and build your solution using exclusively the tools acquired in your auction portfolio.
  • Jury Defense: Pitch, demonstrate, and justify your technical architecture and credit strategy before the judging panel.

02 Rules & Guidelines

  • Squad Size: 2 to 4 members per registered squad.
  • Virtual credits are non-transferable and expire at auction floor closure.
  • Teams must build functional prototypes using only acquired technology items.
  • Evaluation Criteria: Strategic Resource Allocation (25%), Solution Architecture (30%), Implementation Completeness (30%), Pitch & Justification (15%).
