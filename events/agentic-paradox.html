01 Choose Your Theme

Select ONE theme during registration. Problem statements are revealed on event day:

  • 01 — Customer Service & Support

    Automate customer queries, complaints handling, sentiment routing, and omnichannel support workflows.

  • 02 — Education & Student Services

    Build intelligent agents for academic assistance, personalized tutoring, and campus student service automation.

  • 03 — Business & Sales Automation

    Automate customer enquiries, CRM lead qualification, competitive intelligence, and business pipeline operations.

  • 04 — HR & Recruitment

    Create agents for candidate screening, interview coordination, onboarding workflows, and internal employee tasks.

  • 05 — IT Help Desk & Technical Support

    Automate system troubleshooting, incident ticket triage, server alerts resolution, and technical support escalation.

02 Tools, Rules & Judging

Permitted Tools & Frameworks

n8n, Flowise, Dify, Python, LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, or any preferred AI automation framework.

  • Squad Size: Exactly 2 to 3 members per registered team.
  • Build Time: 1 Hour 30 Minutes. Live Demo: 3–5 Minutes per team.
  • Pre-built solutions for the given problem are strictly prohibited.
  • Teams must demonstrate a functional, executing agent that can think, decide, and act.
  • Judging Pillars: Problem Solving • Agentic Behaviour • Functionality • Automation • Innovation • Presentation & Demo.
