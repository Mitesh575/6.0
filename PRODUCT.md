# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students, engineers, and tech enthusiasts participating in the AI & Data Science symposium.

## Product Purpose
Event registration and hype landing page for AIRO 6.0, an annual symposium by the Department of AI & Data Science at Sri Sairam Engineering College. Success means conveying a premium technical experience and converting visitors into registered participants for the 6 arenas.

## Positioning
An immersive, cyberpunk-inspired technical competition ("Six Trials. One Transformation") that tests practical AI, data science, and engineering skills under pressure, distinguishing itself from standard college hackathons with high-stakes formats like live tech auctions and mid-round twists.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The product must present six specific technical arenas (Tech Auction, Tech Crime Scene, Agentic Paradox, Prompt-to-Product, AI Pitch, VibeCraft) with their respective team sizes and prizes.

## Brand Commitments
Name: AIRO 6.0.
Voice: High-stakes, intense, and futuristic ("Bid smart, build smarter", "Digital forensics under pressure").
Visual constraints: Dark mode, cinematic, with cyan (`#00F0FF`) and purple (`#B500FF`) accents.

Event tracks, team sizes, and specific prize amounts (e.g., ₹6,000 for Tech Auction, ₹8,000 for Agentic Paradox) are factual constraints. Contact email: hod.aids@sairam.edu.in. Date: September 26, 2026.
